### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Software Development Engineer** with **2.5+ years of enterprise experience**, specializing in building resilient full-stack applications, cloud-native architectures, and AI-powered solutions. Currently working at **Cyient** (deputed on-site to **Bosch Global Software Technologies**), following a strong tenure delivering BFSI platforms at **LTIMindtree**.

- 🚀 **Current Focus:** Engineering scalable **.NET & Angular** applications, **Azure** cloud integrations, and **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** workflows.
- 🏗️ **Architecture & Craft:** Passionate about **Clean Architecture**, **SOLID principles**, microservices, and high-throughput RESTful APIs.
- 🧠 **Continuous Growth:** Deep-diving into **System Design (LLD/HLD)**, **Design Patterns**, **Data Structures & Algorithms in C#**, and **AI/ML** foundations.
- 🤝 **Open to:** Collaborating on full-stack .NET/Angular projects, cloud-native tooling, and AI engineering.
